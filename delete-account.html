KotaMart – Account & Data Deletion

KotaMart users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your KotaMart account, send an email to:

YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL

Use the subject:

KotaMart Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from the email address associated with your KotaMart account and include the email address registered with the account.

What data will be deleted?

After verification of the request, KotaMart may delete data associated with your account, including:

  • Account profile information
  • Name and contact information
  • Account identifiers
  • Associated service requests and application data where applicable
  • Uploaded account-related information where applicable

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process valid account deletion requests within 30 days.

Contact

For questions regarding account or data deletion, contact: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL
