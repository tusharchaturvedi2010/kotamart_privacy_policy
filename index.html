KotaMart Privacy Policy

Committed to protecting the digital privacy and security of Kota coaching students, residents, and local vendors.

Application ID: com.aistudio.kotamart.ndgwzq • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to KotaMart ("we", "our", or "us"). KotaMart is an integrated city-life digital platform designed specifically for students, coaching aspirants (IIT-JEE / NEET), hostellers, PG owners, mess/tiffin providers, laundry vendors, and auto/cab drivers across Kota, Rajasthan (including Landmark City, Talwandi, Rajeev Gandhi Nagar, Kunhari, Coral Park, Vigyan Nagar, and Indra Vihar).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the KotaMart mobile application and associated online services.

Our Core Privacy Promise: KotaMart will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers or telemarketers. Your information is collected solely to power your coaching city experience, process your service bookings, and ensure student safety.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, as well as data gathered automatically when operating the app:

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Student Account Profile: Full name, verified email address, mobile phone number, coaching institute (e.g., Allen, Resonance, Motion, Reliable, Physics Wallah), and target exam (e.g., JEE Main/Advanced, NEET-UG).
  • Business & Vendor Details: Business name, owner name, service category (PG/Hostel, Mess/Tiffin, Laundry, Daily Needs, Water, Gym, Library, Repair), business address, phone number, and listing photos.
  • Driver Partner Information: Driver name, mobile number, vehicle registration number, auto/cab type, and operating areas.
  • Orders & Inquiries: Mess subscriptions, Golden Token passes, PG room inquiries, laundry pickups, cab booking requests, and support messages.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device & Network Data: Android device model, operating system version, app version, unique Firebase instance ID, and network connection status.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver instant updates on rides, meal check-ins, and booking confirmations.

3. Android Device Permissions

KotaMart requests only minimum necessary Android permissions to deliver requested user features:

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used to calculate walking/driving distances to coaching campuses and discover nearby verified PGs/Tiffin centers, as well as providing live GPS routing during auto/cab bookings. Location is only accessed during active use and is never tracked silently in the background.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively when you explicitly choose to take a photo inside the app (such as scanning a Mess Golden Token QR code or uploading a PG room picture).
  • Storage / Media Picker: Utilizes the zero-permission Android Photo Picker to let you safely choose profile photos or business banner images without granting broad storage access.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to send timely alerts regarding driver arrival, meal attendance, and booking status changes.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Authentication & Security: Creating your account with Firebase Authentication, verifying your email address, and protecting against unauthorized access.
  • Service Delivery: Facilitating room bookings, tiffin meal passes, laundry order tracking, and auto/cab ride dispatching.
  • Golden Token QR System: Enabling secure QR-based meal attendance verification for student mess memberships.
  • Customer Support: Responding to student inquiries, resolving service complaints, and investigating safety issues.

5. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

KotaMart leverages enterprise-grade cloud providers to deliver fast and reliable services:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, FCM): For encrypted user authentication, real-time database sync, cloud photo persistence, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps & OpenStreetMap APIs: For accurate coaching institute distance calculation and city navigation in Kota.

These third-party service providers process data in strict accordance with industry privacy standards and under confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your profile data as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control of your digital identity:

  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and associated profile records directly within the app under Settings → Delete Account or by emailing our privacy team.
  • Data Export: You can request a summary copy of your stored profile information.

7. Security Safeguards

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit, strict Firestore security rules preventing cross-user data leakage, and secure tokenized authentication.
